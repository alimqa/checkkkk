Diploma project

SmartHome IoT: a smart home that predicts motion

Astana IT University, April 2026. Team of three. Defended 95/100.

A sensor node on an ESP8266 board sends temperature, humidity, light, motion and air quality to the cloud. A machine learning model predicts motion two minutes ahead, so the light can switch on before someone walks in. A phone app shows live data, alerts and a sustainability score tied to four UN Sustainable Development Goals.

  • ArchitectureI designed the system end to end: sensors, cloud, ML and phone app.
  • Motion predictionRandom Forest model with 95.3% accuracy, trained on data from a real room.
  • Personal comfortAlert thresholds adapt to how the user actually behaves.
  • Anomaly detectionZ-score checks on temperature, humidity, air quality and light.
  • CostComponents cost under $30. I also calculated the unit economics.
  • Real-world testWhile we set up the diploma exhibition, the air quality sensor caught spikes from glue and printing materials, and the app sent an alert.
